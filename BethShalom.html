Our Sacred Foundation

Founded on Unwavering Compassion & Saintly Grace

His Grace Kuriakose Mar Gregorios of blessed memory, affectionately known across Kerala as ‘Pampady Thirumeni’, led a holy and saintly life centered at Mar Kuriakose Dayara, Pampady. He embodied boundless empathy, love, and protection towards the elderly, the differently abled, and the marginalized in our society.

Carrying this venerable mantle forward, BethShalom Geevarghese Mar Ivanios (GMI) Retirement Home was birthed from the heartfelt vision of Late Lamented His Grace Geevarghese Mar Ivanios Thirumeni. It stands as a beacon of safety for children and adults with developmental delays alongside their aging parents.

Functioning under the steadfast governance of PMGM Abhayabhavan Charitable Society (Reg. No. K. 20 of 1967), the sanctuary unites geriatric-friendly living spaces, round-the-clock supportive nursing, and spiritual nourishment within the Kottayam Diocese.

format_quote

"To comfort those who weep and to extend both hands to those whom the world overlooks—this is pure worship."

— Saintly Memory of H.G. Kuriakose Mar Gregorios (Pampady Thirumeni)

His Grace Kuriakose Mar Gregorios of blessed memory, affectionately revered across Kerala as ‘Pampady Thirumeni’, was a figure of incandescent empathy. During an era when those living with severe bodily ailments and intellectual challenges were frequently marginalized, Thirumeni transformed Mar Kuriakose Dayara in Pothenpuram into an open sanctuary of unceasing prayer, healing, and warmth.

Following his departure to the heavenly realm, his disciples and devotees established the PMGM Abhayabhavan Charitable Society in 1965 at the Dayara. Their single guiding principle was to institutionalize his tender solicitude into permanent physical homes where love and dignity could flourish undisturbed.

visibility The GMI Inspiration

Answering the Silent Prayer of Every Aging Caregiver.

In traditional senior care, aging parents are severed from their adult children who require specialized support due to cerebral palsy, Down syndrome, severe autism, or cognitive impairment. Mar Ivanios recognized this tragic flaw in modern societal care structures.

He designated Beth Shalom GMI as an innovative, dual-purpose community where parents receive top-tier geriatric comfort, companionship, and round-the-clock medical care, while their children access dedicated behavioral therapy, compassionate caregivers, and agricultural vocational empowerment within the exact same building and campus.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information regarding daily life, clinical emergency preparedness, and family engagement.

family_restroom Can family members accompany residents or visit frequently? expand_more
Yes, warm family bonds are deeply nurtured at Beth Shalom. Relatives and children may visit during regular daytime hours. For overseas or out-of-station family members arriving in Kerala, guest dining and scheduled short-stay visitor arrangements can be facilitated with prior notification to the superintendent.
medical_services How is medical emergency care managed around the clock? expand_more
The retirement home maintains trained on-campus nursing staff, round-the-clock emergency medical response, oxygen support, and vital signs monitoring. In addition, Beth Shalom has an institutional tie-up with premier multi-speciality tertiary hospitals in Pampady and Kottayam town with designated on-call ambulance conveyance.
description What documents are required during formal admission? expand_more
Standard documentation includes: (1) Government Identity & Address proof of the resident and nominated local/NRI guardian (Aadhaar, Passport, or Voter ID), (2) Comprehensive medical history summary and latest physician prescriptions, and (3) Two recent passport-sized photographs.
accessible_forward Does Beth Shalom support differently-abled children and adults? expand_more
Yes, this is an integral pillar of our founding mission under the vision of Late Lamented His Grace Geevarghese Mar Ivanios Thirumeni. We welcome developmentally challenged individuals, adults with autism or cerebral palsy, and offer integrated options where elderly parents and their special-needs children reside harmoniously under unified institutional protection.
hotel Are short-term or convalescent respite stays permitted? expand_more
Subject to room availability in our Nest and Heaven suites, we accommodate short-term respite stays (minimum 30 days) for seniors recovering post-surgery or whose primary caregivers are temporarily traveling abroad.

train

accessible Is the campus wheelchair accessible?

Yes, Beth Shalom is engineered strictly as a geriatric-friendly facility. We have zero-threshold entry ramps, wide sensor lifts, grab-bar corridors, and specialized wheelchairs available at our welcome reception.

meeting_room Can families view furnished suite models?

local_taxi Can pick-up from Kottayam station be arranged?

Yes. For visiting senior citizens or NRI family representatives arriving from distant locations, we can coordinate trusted local transport to and from Kottayam Railway Station or bus stands with prior notice.
